Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about PDF MASTER and how to use our tools effectively.

What is PDF MASTER? +
PDF MASTER is a web-based application offering a suite of PDF tools including merging, compressing, converting, and OCR.
Is PDF MASTER free to use? +
Yes, PDF MASTER offers free access to all its core features without any hidden charges.
Do I need to create an account? +
No account is required. You can use all tools anonymously.
How do I merge PDF files? +
Navigate to the Merge tool, upload your PDF files, arrange them in the desired order, and click "Merge".
Can I compress PDFs without losing quality? +
Yes, our compression tool reduces file size while maintaining good visual quality.
What image formats are supported for conversion? +
We support JPG, PNG, BMP, and TIFF formats for image to PDF conversion.
Are my files safe? +
Yes, all files are encrypted during transfer and deleted from our servers after processing.
Do you share my data with third parties? +
No, we respect your privacy and do not share your data.
What if a tool is not working? +
Please try refreshing the page or clearing your browser cache. Contact us if the problem persists.
Which browsers are supported? +
PDF MASTER works best on modern browsers like Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari.
